Common questions

Before you come in

Do I need an appointment for the OPD?
No. Walk-in patients are seen in turn on a token system. Booking a slot simply shortens the wait, and the emergency room never requires an appointment.
Is the emergency room really open at night?
Yes. A duty doctor, nursing staff, the laboratory and the pharmacy are all staffed 24 hours a day, every day of the year.
How long do lab reports take?
Most routine tests are ready the same evening. Cultures and specialised panels take two to three days. We send reports by WhatsApp if you ask at the sample counter.
Can a female doctor see me?
A female doctor or nurse is on duty at all hours. Tell reception when you register and you will be seen by a female clinician.
Do you collect samples from home?
Yes, within I-14, I-15, I-16 and G-14. Book before 09:00 for same-day collection. A small travel charge applies outside these sectors.
What if I cannot afford treatment?
Ask reception about the Zakat-funded assistance scheme. Applications are reviewed by the hospital committee and no one is turned away from emergency care over payment.
